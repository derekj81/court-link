First time: the record button
  1. Android Settings, Connected devices, Pair new device: pick CourtLink. That lets the M5Paper press the camera's shutter, like a selfie remote.
  2. In the camera app's settings, set the volume keys to take pictures or record video.
  3. Before each game: camera in Video mode with the 0.6x lens. The M5Paper asks you to check, because it can't see the phone's screen.
  4. Then press Connect here once to load your pack. During play the M5Paper works on its own; Court Link only needs to be open to send packs and collect results.
